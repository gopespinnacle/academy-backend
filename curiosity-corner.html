What is Curiosity Corner?

Curiosity Corner is the daily learning platform of Gopes Pinnacle Academy. It is designed to inspire students to explore something new every single day. Learning should never stop when the classroom ends. Every day brings an opportunity to discover a powerful vocabulary word, an amazing science fact, a challenging brain teaser, an inspiring life lesson, or a fascinating piece of world knowledge.

Our goal is to develop curiosity, creativity, confidence, communication skills, scientific thinking, logical reasoning, and lifelong learning habits in every student.
